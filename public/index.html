How the Command Centre works

  
What it is. One place on your phone for every Claude chat, question and to-do across all 7 projects. Every chat adds itself when it starts and when you wrap it up.

  
Bottom bar. Home = today at a glance. Chats = your chats sorted into lists. Tasks = questions and to-dos. Projects = one card per project. Apps = quick links, new chats and all your apps.

  
The four buttons on a chat only sort the chat into a list. Nothing is deleted and nothing happens in Claude:

  
• Doing now: set automatically. Any chat that posted to the board in the last 3 hours shows as Doing now, and drops back to Open after 3 quiet hours. You can also tap it yourself.
• Watch list: parked, but you want to keep an eye on it (Chats > Watching).
• Open: normal, still going.
• Done: finished. Moves to Chats > Done for 14 days.

  
Pressed the wrong one? Tap Undo on the message at the bottom, or just tap the right button.

  
Open chat takes you straight back to that chat. Wrap up copies a wrap-up message and opens the chat: paste and send. Do it on a question or to-do copies a ready-made opener and opens the right chat or project.

  
Ticking the box on a question or to-do moves it to Tasks > Ticked off (tap again to bring it back).

  
Green spinning line = Claude is busy on a long job in that chat right now.

  
NEEDS WRAP-UP = you worked in that chat after its last wrap-up.

  
Looks finished? (Chats page, and a Tidy up banner on Home) = chats quiet for 7 days with nothing open. Check the list and tap Mark all as Done. Undo is there if you change your mind.

  
Close a chat from Claude: type "close this chat" in the chat. It wraps up and marks itself Done here.

  
Sync (Projects page): opens a new chat in that project that posts any of its chats missing from the board. Use it when a project looks out of date. A chat can only see chats in its own project, so there is one Sync per project.

  
Rename next to a chat title. Use: date - area - job, for example 22 Oct - ZS - Michah invoices.

  
+ Add starts a new chat in a project or adds a note. The drop-down at the top shows one project only.

  
Waiting on someone = a note type for things you are waiting on Binghi, Ben Warren, a client and so on. Tasks > Waiting on lists them by person with how long you have waited. Do it drafts a chase. Tick it when they come back to you.

  
Edit on any question or to-do changes its wording, type, due date, who you are waiting on, or its project.

  
Search box on Home searches chats, questions, to-dos, waiting-on items and apps all at once.

  
Week in review (Tasks page, and a banner on Home from Friday) shows what got done, what was added, what is overdue and which projects went quiet in the last 7 days.

  
07:00 email: on weekdays you get one email at 07:00 only if something is overdue or due today. No email means nothing is late.
